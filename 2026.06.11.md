# Science and engineering

One of my recent concern is my major. Though I already know that I want to become a 'leader in pharmaceutical and biotech field', I also found out that there are numerous ways to achieve that. I can major in chemical engineering, graduate MS in the same field, get MBA, and continue my career not as the R&D scientist or engineer but as the pharmacy firm analyst or VC manager. Or, I can major in chemistry (or chemical engineering), graduate Phd in chemical engineering, and work as a scientist or an engineer in the drug discovery field or biotech. I prefer the latter as I have deep interest in the science and engineering. I also found out that what I consequently want is the engineering: I do not just want to find the mechanism of some chemical reaction or drug intake but to create the system of the drug or treatment that can save millions of people.

However, in the field of pharmacy and biotech, science and engineering are both needed for the successful novel drug and system. Creating the product itself without knowing the exact mechanism is crucial, but I also have to understand the foundational knowledge in chemistry, biology, and physics for the complete research and its application.
