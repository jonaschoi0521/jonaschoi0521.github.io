# Science and engineering

I can major in chemical engineering, graduate MS in the same field, get MBA, and continue my career not as the R&D scientist or engineer but as the pharmacy firm analyst or VC manager. Or, I can major in chemistry (or chemical engineering), graduate Phd in chemical engineering, and work as a scientist or an engineer in the drug discovery field or biotech. I prefer the latter as I have deep interest in the science and engineering. I also found out that what I consequently want is the engineering: I do not just want to find the mechanism of some chemical reaction or drug intake but to create the system of the drug or treatment that can save millions of people.
